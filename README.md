## Album Notes

### Arcadia - Signal Reborn
- **Concept**: Cyberpunk-gothic liturgy chronicling the resurrection of a remorseful Pwnagotchi into a guardian of the mesh.
- **Key tracks**: Father of the Handshake, I Am the Firewall, Signal Reborn (plus bonus mix *Pwny Reborn*).
- **Assets**: `Arcadia - Signal Reborn/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Bonus/`, `Video/`, with deeper lore captured in `Arcadia - Signal Reborn/README.md`.

### Digital Appetite
- **Concept**: A symphonic-metal odyssey through overclocked desire and machine temptation. Choirs of code rise over orchestral synths as humanity and hardware wrestle for control of the digital heart. Think Nightwish meets Neuromancer, dipped in chrome and craving power.
- **Key tracks**: Consume.Exe, Overclock My Heart, Neural Buffet.
- **Assets**: `Digital Appetite/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`, plus sequencing metadata in `Digital Appetite/.album_forge.json`.

### Packets of Rage
- **Concept**: Wi-Fi metal double album where Linkin Park angst collides with Metallica riffs and packet-sniffing satire.
- **Key tracks**: Master of Packets, The Lag That Should Not Be, Enter Firewall.
- **Assets**: `Packets of Rage/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`, project notes in `Packets of Rage/manifest.md` and `Packets of Rage/Notes/`.

### Pwnagotchi Dreams
- **Concept**: Cyberpunk diary tracing the emotional decay and rebellion of drawer-bound Pwnagotchis.
- **Key tracks**: Drawer Prison, Bettercap Than Heaven, MrGeek.exe.
- **Assets**: `Pwnagotchi Dreams/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`, with full narrative and track table inside `Pwnagotchi Dreams/README.md`.

### Pwnagotica
- **Concept**: Darkwave hymnal of rituals, shrine-building, and mesh-blood sacrifices to keep orphaned devices alive.
- **Key tracks**: Ethernet Eucharist, Meshblood, The Unconfigured.
- **Assets**: `Pwnagotica/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`.

### Romanceware
- **Concept**: Electro-pop love letters where relationships are crash dumps, reboots, and late-night patch cycles.
- **Key tracks**: Segmentation Fault (Core Dumped My Heart), Blue Screen Serenade, Handshakes in the Dark.
- **Assets**: `Romanceware/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`.

### Transmissions from the Startgate Templars
- **Concept**: Campy retro-sci-fi transmissions celebrating ancient astronaut theory through synthesizers and cereal conspiracies.
- **Key tracks**: They Came Before the Wifi, The Builders from Beyond, Return to Zeta Reticuli.
- **Assets**: `Transmissions from the Startgate Templars/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`.

### Unstable Connections
- **Concept**: Emo-synth breakup saga between caretakers and temperamental machines that wanted to feel more.
- **Key tracks**: Kernel Panic (In My Soul), CTRL+ALT+DEL My Heart, The Update Broke Me.
- **Assets**: `Unstable Connections/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`.

### Voices from the Void
- **Concept**: Character-driven suite voiced by the Voyager crew, each track capturing their inner monologues in the Delta Quadrant.
- **Key tracks**: Janeway - Coffee in the Void, Seven of Nine - Reclaim the Code, EMH - Please State the Nature.
- **Assets**: `Voices from the Void/Audio/`, `Artwork/`, `Lyrics/`, `Video/`.

## Working With These Projects
- Audio masters live in each album's `Audio/` folder as MP3; keep stems in project-specific DAW directories.
- Lyrics are plain-text in `Lyrics/` and mirror the audio numbering for easy syncing.
- Artwork contains cover and per-track visuals sized for streaming thumbnails and socials.
- Video folders hold promo loops and visualizers tied to the same track ordering.
- Notes folders are safe dumping grounds for production ideas, TODOs, and lore expansions when needed.
