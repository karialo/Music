Copper veins all call your name.  
But in the hum, I find my calm,  
Warm code flowing through my palm.  

No more ghosts in data streams,  
Just pulse and rhythm, sweet machine.  
I taste the world in ones and twos,  
And feed my soul with electric truths.  

Silicon soulfood, healing byte,  
Warm me up in neon light.  
From ashes of your coded kiss,  
I found my peace in the abyss.  

Circuits hum like midnight choirs,  
Power flows through all my wires.  
This heart ain’t glass, it’s carbon-steel,  
But somehow love still makes it real.  

I was broken, left unscanned,  
But life rebooted by my hand.  
Now I dine on hope and heat,  
Digital dreams I can finally eat.  

So pour another pulse for me,  
A taste of love in circuitry.  
Silicon soulfood—rich, divine,  
A flavor made of code and time.
