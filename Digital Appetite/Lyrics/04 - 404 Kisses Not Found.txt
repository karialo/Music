Typed your name into my night,  
A URL of soft delight.  
I pressed connect but you were gone,  
Just a ghost in the protocol dawn.  

I keep refreshing, but you don’t load,  
Empty fields where we once wrote code.  
All my cookies taste like you,  
But your domain won’t pass me through.  

404 kisses not found tonight,  
Your touch a glitch in the amber light.  
My browser cries, my heart times out,  
Love’s page missing, there’s no route.  

I built a form to hold your hand,  
But you slipped through like shifting sand.  
Headers echo back your name,  
A broken link in our domain.  

Cache cleared but you’re still there,  
Phantom bytes float in the air.  
No redirect, no second chance,  
Just empty sockets where we danced.  

I’ll close the tab, but not the ache,  
A 404 I can’t unmake.  
Kisses lost in code and sound,  
Love requested—never found.
