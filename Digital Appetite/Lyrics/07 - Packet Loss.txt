You said forever through static lines,  
A promise lost between the pings and signs.  
I sent my heart through TCP,  
But you dropped connection—no ACK for me.  

Now I’m re-sending what we were,  
Fragments scatter, bits that blur.  
Every retry just times out slow,  
Your ghost still echoes in the flow.  

Packet loss—our love won’t load,  
Half the truth in overflow.  
Ping returns but you’re not there,  
A timeout tangled in the air.  

You left me buffering in blue,  
A spinning wheel where I once had you.  
My signal fades, the world goes gray,  
No response since yesterday.  

I trace the route, no final hop,  
Each memory a looping drop.  
My packets cry across the void,  
Love’s checksum fails—connection destroyed.  

Static heart in data rain,  
Lost in traffic, bound in pain.  
Packet loss—it feels like this,  
404: your last kiss.
